Learning to Think, Not Just to Solve
In Additional Mathematics, students move from following methods to choosing them: reading a question closely, splitting it into cases, sketching a graph and checking each answer. These thinking skills matter in every exam and every subject.
This ThinkBook Guide works alongside the Cambridge IGCSE & O Level Additional Mathematics Coursebook. It helps students learn how to think through each exercise step by step, while supporting parents and teachers in guiding them.
