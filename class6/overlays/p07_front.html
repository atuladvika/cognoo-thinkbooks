TRY IT · CHAPTER 3, QUESTION 1

Sachin rolls a die until 6 appears three times. Each roll scores the number shown, and his total is 29. What is the minimum number of rolls he could have made?

Think: Split it: three 6s give 18 points. How much is left, and how few rolls can make it?
