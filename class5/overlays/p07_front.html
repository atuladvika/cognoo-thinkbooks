TRY IT · CHAPTER 2, QUESTION 10

Four buyers come into a chocolate shop one by one. Each buys half of the chocolates left. The fourth buyer buys only 1 chocolate. How many were there at first?

Think: Work one buyer at a time, going backwards: before the 4th buyer there were 2. How many before the 3rd?

TRY IT · CHAPTER 2, QUESTION 9

Six times me makes a whole and a half. Take me out of a whole and three of me are left. Who am I?

Think: Say it simply: “Six of me make one and a half.”
