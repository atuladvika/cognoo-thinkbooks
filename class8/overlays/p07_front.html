TRY IT · CHAPTER 2, QUESTION 7

How many possible combinations of 1 owl and 1 tree can be made from 3 owls and 5 trees?

Think: Take one owl at a time. How many trees can it be paired with? Then add up for all three owls.

Sam has two different two-digit numbers. Both are divisible by 9, and neither is a multiple of 18. What is the LEAST possible sum of the two numbers?

Think: Say it simply: “two-digit multiples of 9 that are not multiples of 18.” List the smallest ones.
