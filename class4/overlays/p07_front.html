TRY IT · CHAPTER 7, QUESTION 7

A container has 20 blue balls and 13 red balls. Each button removes some balls. Sam presses Red, Green, Blue, then Green. Which balls are left?

Think: Do one press at a time. Write the blue and the red count after every press, then move to the next button.

TRY IT · CHAPTER 8, QUESTION 6

Johnny can carry 20 kg in all and already has a bag. How can he find the extra weight he can carry?

Think: Say it simply: “20 kg is the bag plus the extra.”
